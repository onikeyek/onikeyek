### 🔮 &nbsp; about me

Hi, I'm **Naimot-Omodolapo Yekini** — a Data Analyst and aspiring Data Engineer with a background spanning Economics, Information Technology, and IoT.

My background in Economics sparked my passion for using data to understand patterns, solve problems, and support decision-making. I later moved into technology, building skills across data analytics, data engineering, and IoT engineering. I enjoy working across the full data lifecycle — from collecting and cleaning data to analysing, transforming, visualising, and building the systems that generate and process it.

I'm also a vibe coder at heart: I build with intention, curiosity, and a playlist in the background. 🎧
